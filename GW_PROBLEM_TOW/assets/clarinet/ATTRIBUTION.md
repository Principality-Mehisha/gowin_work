# Clarinet reference recordings

Source: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples.

- Instrument page: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISBbclarinet.html
- Collection and usage statement: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
- Creator of the collection: Lawrence Fritts and the Electronic Music Studios contributors.
- Performer listed for this clarinet session: Christine Bellomy.
- Instrument listed: Buffet R13, Morre reed and Glenn Bowen B4 mouthpiece.
- Session: August 3, 1998, anechoic chamber, Neumann KM 84 microphone.
- Downloaded: 2026-10-25.

The official collection page states that the recordings may be downloaded and
used for projects without restrictions. Preserve this source credit and the
source URLs with future fitted parameters.

All 12 chromatic-scale files were downloaded: pp, mf and ff at D3–B3,
C4–B4, C5–B5 and C6–B6. Offline analysis retains sounding D3–Bb6,
45 notes at three dynamics (135 profiles), excluding the unused B6.

Downloaded files are unmodified 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, mono AIFF recordings. Exact
URLs, byte counts and SHA256 hashes are recorded in `source_manifest.json`.
`measurements.json` stores the segmented-note spectral measurements;
`fit_report.json` describes the generated 32-partial attack/sustain and
envelope/noise parameters in `src/rom/clarinet45*.hex`. Expression contours
and small pitch fluctuations are separately designed performance controls.
The recordings are offline analysis references, not FPGA PCM ROM contents
or SD playback inputs. The FPGA synthesizes every output sample in real time.

Supplementary scientific reference:
https://phys.unsw.edu.au/music/clarinet/index.html . UNSW note-page names use
written Bb-clarinet pitches, while its frequency plots show sounding pitch;
account for that transposition when cross-checking against MIDI.
